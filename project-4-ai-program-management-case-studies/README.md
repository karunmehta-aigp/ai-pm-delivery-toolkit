AI Program Management is about much more than managing scope, schedule, and budget. Success depends on asking the right questions, understanding the type of AI initiative, establishing the right governance, managing AI-specific risks, and defining meaningful success metrics from the very beginning.

This one-page reference highlights the some key considerations for initiating and delivering enterprise AI programs:

 ✅ Types of AI programs and delivery models
 ✅ Technical literacy for AI Program Managers
 ✅ AI governance approval gates
 ✅ AI Program Initiation Checklist
 ✅ AI-specific challenges and practical solutions
 ✅ Success metrics that matter

AI Program Managers don't need to build AI models—they need to lead AI initiatives with the right strategy, governance, stakeholders, and execution to deliver trusted, scalable, and measurable business outcomes.
